Rule-Based Expert System Architecture

MediBot models clinical reasoning using classical Artificial Intelligence techniques. It relies on explicit IF-THEN conditional rules and dual-mode inference engines rather than statistical probability.

1. Knowledge Base

Contains structured domain expertise stored as explicit IF-THEN conditional rules connecting symptom patterns to diagnostic hypotheses.

2. Fact Base

Stores active clinical facts reported by the user during the diagnostic assessment session in working memory.

3. Inference Engine

Executes Forward Chaining to derive conclusions from facts, and Backward Chaining to verify specific target hypotheses.
